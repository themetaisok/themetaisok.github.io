Research and data

I wrote the data standard behind DefiLlama’s real-world-asset coverage. Pantera Capital, Citi Institute, and CoinGecko cite the datasets I built and the metrics I defined.

  • Sole author of the public methodology: eligibility and exclusion criteria, metrics, taxonomy, the perps spec, the treasury methodology, and a 35-column data dictionary, as of November 2026.
  • A registry of more than 9,000 assets and markets across 116 fields, 450+ issuers, 330+ platforms, and 170+ blockchains, as of September 2026.
  • Six measures defined, from Active Market Cap to three variants of mNAV.
  • The finding: under 20% of the tokens marketed as real-world assets clear the bar I wrote, as of September 2026.
Seats: senior research analyst, research lead, head of research, and methodology or data product owner.

Does not count: A number without its definition.

RWA partnerships and institutional growth

Figure, Securitize, Ondo, and xStocks came to me for listing. I know what issuers need, and I know where their numbers break.

  • Each supplied its data against my schema, and inbound requests replaced outbound research as the main driver of coverage growth.
  • The registry I built spans 450+ issuers and 330+ platforms, as of September 2026.
  • I brokered the data partnership with the RWA Foundation, which uses DefiLlama’s RWA perpetuals data in its own reporting.
  • I wrote the criteria issuers are measured against. In September 2025, I ran the assessment when DefiLlama declined to list an asset claiming $10B+.
Seats: tokenization lead, RWA partnerships, ecosystem RWA lead, and institutional growth.

Does not count: A listing on request. Every asset was run against the same published criteria.

Product for data and tokenization products

I specified three production dashboards from an empty sheet: scope, metrics, schema, documentation, and launch.

  • All three are live: the RWA dashboard, the RWA perpetuals tracker, and the digital asset treasuries tracker.
  • A 34-field market schema and a canonical market ID keep the same contract comparable across venues: 1,000+ active onchain markets, and 3,300+ with centralized exchanges, as of September 2026.
  • The fee and revenue methodology made issuer economics comparable. Every estimate carries a confidence grade and a dated primary-source trail.
  • Before DefiLlama, Crypto Stock Tracker went from concept to live product in about a month in 2025. DefiLlama acqui-hired me on the strength of it.
Seats: product manager for data, reporting, tokenization, or vaults.

Does not count: A spec nobody could build from. DefiLlama’s engineers built all three dashboards to mine.

Digital asset treasuries

I built the tracker covering nearly 100 treasury companies, as of 13 November 2026, and replaced the single mNAV figure with three.

  • Realized, Realistic, and Max mNAV, published as DefiLlama’s treasury methodology. Each one counts a different set of shares.
  • The worked example: Greenlane Holdings on 30 November 2025 was quoted at 0.33×. Count its contractual dilution and it sits at 2.42×.
  • Seven of 96 companies sit below 1.0× on one count and above it on another, as of 13 November 2026.
  • The Digital Asset Treasury Playbook, DL News, October 2025, syndicated to Yahoo Finance.
Seats: treasury-company research, corporate development, and investor relations.

Does not count: The single figure the market was trading on.
