Research and data

I wrote the data standard behind DefiLlama’s real-world-asset coverage. Citi Institute sized the tokenization market on the dataset I built, and Pantera Capital adopted DeFi Active TVL, a metric I defined.

  • Sole author of the public methodology: five inclusion and six exclusion criteria, seven evidence flags, five access models, a 34-field market schema, the treasury methodology, and a 35-column data dictionary (November 2026).
  • Built the registry from an empty sheet to 9,000+ assets and markets, 116 fields each, across 450+ issuers, 330+ platforms, and 170+ blockchains (21 August 2026); it more than tripled in five months.
  • Defined six measures, from Active Market Cap to three variants of mNAV; Pantera Capital, CoinGecko, and CryptoSlate report in them.
  • Established that under 20% of tokens marketed as real-world assets clear the four-part bar I wrote: 1,664 of 9,027 on 21 August 2026.
Bring me in when your numbers need a definition that survives an argument, or a dataset built from an empty sheet.

Does not count: A number without its definition.

  • All three are live: the RWA dashboard, the RWA perpetuals tracker, and the digital asset treasuries tracker. They now measure $34.41 billion in tokenized real-world assets and $13.35 billion in RWA perpetuals open interest (13 November 2026).

Digital asset treasuries

DefiLlama hired me to build its treasuries tracker. I created the dataset behind it, 98 listed companies on 13 November 2026, and defined the three mNAV variants it publishes.

  • Defined Realized, Realistic, and Max mNAV in place of the single figure the market quoted, each counting a different set of shares; published as DefiLlama’s treasury methodology.
  • Proved it in the published worked example: Greenlane Holdings read 0.33× as quoted and 2.42× once contractual dilution was counted (30 November 2025), a premium that had been read as a discount.
  • Traced every purchase to its primary source, with 119 Strategy transactions reconciled to SEC EDGAR (August 2026).
  • Seven of the 96 that report holdings sit below 1.0× on one count and above it on another, as of 13 November 2026.
  • Wrote two mNAV explainers for DL News, The Digital Asset Treasury Playbook (October 2025) and What is mNAV? (September 2025), both syndicated to Yahoo Finance.
Bring me in when the market quotes one mNAV and you need to know what the company is really worth per share.

Does not count: The single figure the market was trading on.
