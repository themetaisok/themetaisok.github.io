RWA dashboard

DefiLlama's view of tokenized real-world assets, generated from a registry I built and maintained. More than 9,000 tracked assets and markets across 116 fields, spanning 450+ issuers, 330+ platforms and 170+ blockchains.

Running the whole registry against the classification bar produced a finding worth stating plainly: fewer than one in five tokens marketed as real-world assets clear the transferable, self-custodial, redeemable and verified test. The rest are better described as programmable finance.

defillama.com/rwa

RWA perpetuals

An aggregate view of real-world-asset perpetuals. Before it the data lived only inside each venue's own front end with no way to compare across them. More than 1,000 active markets in the onchain default view and over 3,300 with centralized exchanges included, covering equities, indices, ETFs, commodities, bonds and forex, normalized to common reference assets through a 17-field market schema and a canonical market ID convention so the same underlying stays comparable across venues that name contracts differently, broken out by venue, asset group and a 75+ market forex book.

defillama.com/rwa/perps

Digital asset treasuries

Nearly 100 listed companies, with every purchase traced to the filing it came from. The market was valuing these companies on a single mNAV figure that hid future share issuance. I replaced it with three defensible variants and showed that a company quoted at 0.33x can sit at 2.42x once contractual dilution is counted.

defillama.com/digital-asset-treasuries

Methodology

Sole author of the public documentation: eligibility and exclusion criteria, metrics, definitions and taxonomy, the perpetuals spec and a standalone treasury methodology, plus a 29-column public data dictionary. Five inclusion criteria, six exclusion criteria, seven evidence flags and five access models, with expulsion rules and a review status short of removal.

Also the fee and revenue methodology that made issuer economics comparable across assets: an identity equation splitting holder fees between asset manager and tokenization platform, a blank-versus-zero rule separating unresearched from verified-no-fee, and every estimate carrying a confidence grade and a dated primary-source audit trail. Primary sources always win over aggregators.

docs.llama.fi/real-world-assets · docs.llama.fi/analysts/dat-methodology

Metrics I defined

Now in use beyond the platform they were built for.

Onchain Market Cap
Circulating supply, excluding burned, at reference price.
Active Market Cap
The portion in user or protocol hands, excluding issuer and custody wallets. The operational test: would compromising this wallet let someone extract meaningful value?
DeFi Active TVL
The subset of Active Market Cap deployed in third-party DeFi protocols, excluding issuer-managed contracts.
Utilization
DeFi Active TVL over Onchain Market Cap, as a percentage.
Net Flows, Flow vs Price, Capital Rotation
Separating minting and redemption from repricing, which for assets like tokenized gold is the only way to read the chart correctly.
mNAV: Realized, Realistic, Maximum
Three treasury-company valuations in place of the single figure the market was trading on, which hid future share issuance.
