Metrics I defined

Definitions, and where each one is used by someone else.

Onchain Market Cap
Onchain Market Cap is circulating supply, excluding burned, at reference price.
Active Market Cap
Active Market Cap is the portion of Onchain Market Cap in user or protocol hands, excluding issuer and custody wallets. The operational test: would compromising this wallet let someone extract meaningful value?Cited in CoinGecko
DeFi Active TVL
DeFi Active TVL is the subset of Active Market Cap deployed in third-party DeFi protocols, excluding issuer-managed contracts.Cited in Pantera Capital and CryptoSlate
Utilization
Utilization is DeFi Active TVL divided by Onchain Market Cap, as a percentage. It is not lending-market utilization, which is borrowed over supplied.
Net Flows, Flow vs Price, Capital Rotation
These separate minting and redemption from repricing. For an asset like tokenized gold, where supply is flat and the price moves, that separation is what tells a flow from a price change.
mNAV: Realized, Realistic, Maximum
mNAV is reported in three variants, Realized, Realistic and Maximum, in place of the single figure the market was trading on, which hid future share issuance.

Built at DefiLlama

August 2025 to September 2026. Zero to one on all three.

RWA dashboard

DefiLlama's view of tokenized real-world assets, generated from a registry I built and maintained. More than 9,000 tracked assets and markets across 116 fields, spanning 450+ issuers, 330+ platforms and 170+ blockchains.

Running the whole registry against the classification bar produced one clear finding: fewer than one in five tokens marketed as real-world assets clear the transferable, self-custodial, redeemable and verified test. The rest are better described as programmable finance.

defillama.com/rwa

RWA perpetuals

An aggregate view of real-world-asset perpetuals. Before it the data lived only inside each venue's own front end with no way to compare across them. It covers equities, indices, ETFs, commodities, bonds and forex, broken out by venue and asset group. Markets are normalized to common reference assets through a 17-field market schema, and each one carries a canonical market ID, so the same underlying stays comparable across venues that name contracts differently.

Digital asset treasuries

Nearly 100 listed companies, with every purchase traced to the filing it came from. These companies were widely quoted on a single mNAV figure that hid future share issuance. I replaced it with three defensible variants and showed that a company quoted at 0.33x can sit at 2.42x once contractual dilution is counted.

defillama.com/digital-asset-treasuries

Methodology

Sole author of the public documentation: eligibility and exclusion criteria, metrics, definitions and taxonomy, the perpetuals spec and a standalone treasury methodology, plus a 29-column public data dictionary. Five inclusion criteria, six exclusion criteria, seven evidence flags and five access models, with expulsion rules and a review status short of removal.

Also the fee and revenue methodology that made issuer economics comparable across assets: an identity equation splitting holder fees between asset manager and tokenization platform, a blank-versus-zero rule separating unresearched from verified-no-fee, and every estimate carrying a confidence grade and a dated primary-source audit trail. Primary sources always win over aggregators.

Strategic Sealed Packz

Co-founder

Sealed Pokemon packs held in vaults and tokenized on Solana, with Magic and sports products planned.

It is a digital asset treasury in everything but the asset. A vehicle holds a reserve, a token trades against it, and a trading fee routes most of its proceeds into buying more of the reserve. There is no redemption right, so a holder gets exposure to the vault rather than a claim on a particular pack.

That structure is the one I measure. An automated engine acts by mNAV band on the gap between the token and the vault, which is the framework I built for listed treasury companies pointed at a shelf of sealed cardboard instead of a balance sheet of crypto. I spent a year building the dashboard that tracks nearly a hundred listed ones before co-founding an unlisted one.

By the classification bar I wrote at DefiLlama it is not a real-world asset, since redeemability is one of the four criteria and there is none here. Treasury vehicle is the accurate label, and it is the one I use.
