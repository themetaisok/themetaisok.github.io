Six measures

Metrics I defined, and where each one is used by someone else.

Six exclusions

Onchain Market Cap

Circulating supply, at reference price.

Does not count: Burned supply.

Active Market Cap

The portion of Onchain Market Cap in user or protocol hands.

The operational test: would compromising this wallet let someone extract meaningful value?

Cited in CoinGecko

Does not count: Issuer wallets. Custody wallets.

DeFi Active TVL

The subset of Active Market Cap deployed in third-party DeFi protocols.

Cited in Pantera Capital and CryptoSlate

Does not count: Issuer-managed contracts.

Utilization

DeFi Active TVL divided by Onchain Market Cap, as a percentage.

Does not count: Lending-market utilization, which is borrowed over supplied.

Net Flows, Flow vs Price, Capital Rotation

Minting and redemption, read apart from price. For an asset like tokenized gold, where supply is flat and the price moves, that separation is what tells a flow from a price change.

Does not count: Repricing.

mNAV

Three variants: Realized, Realistic and Maximum.

Does not count: The single figure the market was trading on, which hid future share issuance.

RWA perpetuals

An aggregate view of real-world-asset perpetuals. It covers equities, indices, ETFs, commodities, bonds and forex, broken out by venue and asset group. Markets are normalized to common reference assets through a 17-field market schema, and each one carries a canonical market ID, so the same underlying stays comparable across venues that name contracts differently.
